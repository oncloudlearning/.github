# OnCloudLearning

**Learn by building. Grow through practice. Prove what you can do.**

OnCloudLearning helps aspiring and developing technology professionals build practical skills through structured learning, realistic engineering scenarios, hands-on labs, collaborative projects, and evidence of real work.

## Odyssey: Engineering experience through practice

Odyssey is our practical engineering experience programme. Participants move beyond passive learning by exploring systems, solving problems, documenting decisions, and developing the habits used by working engineers.

### Odyssey: GroundZero

GroundZero is our foundations pathway. Participants develop essential engineering workflows and learn through guided tasks involving Linux, Windows Server, networking, Git, command-line tools, troubleshooting, and realistic project environments.

### Learn through realistic engineering work

Our learning approach emphasises:

- **Explore:** Understand a running environment before making changes.
- **Investigate:** Gather evidence and reason about technical problems.
- **Build:** Work with tools, repositories, infrastructure, and repeatable workflows.
- **Collaborate:** Use branches, commits, pull requests, reviews, and clear technical communication.
- **Document:** Record findings, decisions, and evidence of completed work.
- **Reflect:** Learn from feedback and improve the quality of engineering work.

### Our simulation environments

Some Odyssey exercises use fictional organisations and simulated technology environments to make engineering work realistic and safe.

**EverCloud Technologies is a fictional organisation used in the Odyssey: GroundZero simulation.** It provides the context for selected scenarios, tickets, applications, and engineering tasks. It is not the owner or operator of OnCloudLearning or the Odyssey programme.

### Getting started

1. Explore the repositories available to you.
2. Read the relevant README and task instructions before beginning.
3. Follow the project's contribution and security guidelines.
4. Record your findings and document your work.
5. Use issues and pull requests where the project workflow requires them.

Our goal is to help learners develop practical competence, sound engineering judgement, and a portfolio of evidence that demonstrates how they approach real technical work.
